Company Due-Diligence Evidence Checklist

A reproducible way to review an Israeli company before a transaction.

  1. Confirm the legal name, company number and status.
  2. Record directors, shareholders and related entities where public data permits.
  3. Review material legal proceedings and recent company changes.
  4. Document each source, its date and its limitations.
  5. Separate verified facts from assumptions and request professional advice where needed.

Research starting points: DataCheck business information · DataCheck OSINT

Informational checklist only; not legal, financial or regulatory advice.
